Duas (duas.json)

Supplications grouped by purpose: forgiveness, worry and distress, guidance
and the istikhara, knowledge, provision and debt, parents and family,
illness, travel, the home, meals and the mosque, loss and grief, and duas
from the Quran.

The Arabic of the duas from the hadith follows Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of
the Muslim), by Sa'id ibn Ali ibn Wahf al-Qahtani. Each names the
collections that report it ("source").

Duas from the Quran are not copied here: "quran" holds a reference, and the
app shows the ayahs from its own Quran text (assets/quran, Tanzil Project).
Their source is "quran".

The English and French meanings, the transliterations ("translit": how a
dua is said, in English and French spelling), and the notes on when to say
it were written for Rafiq. They are drafts for native review, like the
adhkar's (assets/adhkar/NOTICE.txt).
