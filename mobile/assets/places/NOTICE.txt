Towns and countries (cities.tsv, countries.tsv)

Source: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), the "cities5000", "countryInfo"
and "alternateNamesV2" dumps of 10 November 2026.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Built by data/build_places.py, which says what is kept: every populated
place of 5,000 people or more that is a town in its own right (sections and
numbered districts of a town, and places abandoned or historical, are left
out), with its name, its
French and Arabic names where GeoNames has them, its country and its
coordinates to three decimals; and each country's English, French and Arabic
names. Invisible direction marks are removed from the names.

Changes from GeoNames, each made on purpose and listed in the script:
- Jerusalem is kept as one entry, placed in Palestine (GeoNames files the
  city under IL and its eastern part, as a section, under PS).
- The English name of PS is "Palestine" (GeoNames: "Palestinian Territory").

The list is used on the phone only: to name the position the phone finds,
and to let someone choose a town by hand. No position is sent anywhere.
