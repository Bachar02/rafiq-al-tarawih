The adhan (android/app/src/main/res/raw/adhan_full.ogg, adhan_takbir.ogg)

Android plays a notification's sound from the app's raw resources, so the
recordings live there and not among the Flutter assets.

Recording: "Beautiful adhan.ogg", by Adam-synagda, 29 April 2022, own work.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_adhan.ogg
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

adhan_full.ogg is the recording itself, byte for byte
(SHA-1 a1fa4fd942401922c5c3301816384aae86956522): the whole adhan, twelve
phrases, 2 min 34 s. adhan_takbir.ogg is its first 31 seconds, the four
opening takbirs, cut in the silence after them without re-encoding
(data/build_adhan.py).

The recording is the general adhan. It is also played for Fajr: no recording
of the Fajr adhan (with "as-salatu khayrun mina n-nawm") under a public-domain
dedication was found.
