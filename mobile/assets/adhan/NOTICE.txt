The adhan (android/app/src/main/res/raw/adhan_full.mp3, adhan_takbir.mp3)

Android plays a notification's sound from the app's raw resources, so the
recordings live there and not among the Flutter assets.

Recording: the adhan chosen by Rafiq's owner for every prayer, Fajr included
(data/adhan/adhan.mp3, SHA-1 db4e5f8390785e544bcdd2a317f68c1709de092e).
Source and licence: TO BE RECORDED HERE before release (who recited it, where
it was published, and the permission or licence that allows Rafiq to ship it).

adhan_full.mp3 is the whole recording (twelve phrases, 2 min 32 s), raised to
a peak of -1 dBFS so a phone plays it loud enough. adhan_takbir.mp3 is its
first 26 seconds, the four opening takbirs, cut in the silence after them
with a short fade. Both are made by data/build_adhan.py.

Earlier builds (0.7 to 0.9) played "Beautiful adhan.ogg" by Adam-synagda,
Wikimedia Commons, CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
