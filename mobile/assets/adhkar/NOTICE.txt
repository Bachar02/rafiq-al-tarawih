Adhkar (adhkar.json)

Selection and Arabic wording: Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim), by
Sa'id ibn Ali ibn Wahf al-Qahtani: the chapters on the morning and the
evening, after the prayer, before sleep, and the excellence of remembrance.
The texts are hadith; each dhikr names the collections that report it
("source"). Counts follow the hadith (for example 33, 33 and 34 before sleep).

Quranic passages (Ayat al-Kursi, al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas, the end of
al-Baqarah) are not copied here: "quran" holds a reference, and the app shows
the ayahs from its own Quran text (assets/quran, Tanzil Project).

The English and French meanings, and the notes on how or when to say a
dhikr, were written for Rafiq. They are drafts for native review, like the
app's other French and Arabic strings (PLAN.md §25).

Morning and evening share one list: "evening" gives the evening's own words
(amsayna for asbahna) and "only" marks a dhikr said at one of the two.
